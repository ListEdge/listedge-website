The opportunity

Half the market is invisible. The buyer sits with one agent; the right property sits with another — and the deal never happens.

ListEdge connects that demand and supply privately, across offices. No portals, no public listings, no client names — just licensed agents, the buyers they're working, and the properties that fit, found for each other.

How it works

One network, matched both ways.

Whether you're holding the buyer or the listing, ListEdge does the same thing — it finds the other side, across every member office.

01

Post the buyer, or the listing

Add an anonymised buyer brief — it works like a listing for them — or list a property privately to the network.

02

Get matched, both ways

ListEdge matches buyers and listings across the network instantly — strong matches and near-misses, with the reasons why.

03

Make the introduction

Whoever holds the match makes the call. Every introduction is logged as a shared, time-stamped conjunctional record.
